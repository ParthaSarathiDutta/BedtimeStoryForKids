Nia sat in her back garden, staring up at the sky. The moon was shining brightly, but it looked different tonight. She furrowed her brow and wondered out loud, "Why does the moon change shape every night?" And so, her quest for answers began.

Nia decided to ask her cat, Whiskers, for his thoughts on the matter. She scooped him up and whispered her question in his ear. Whiskers purred softly and nuzzled her cheek before giving his guess. "Maybe the moon changes shape because it's playing a game with the stars," he mewed. Nia giggled at the idea of the moon playing games in the night sky. She thanked Whiskers for his guess and set off to ask someone else for their thoughts.

Next, Nia went to ask her wise old owl friend, Hootie, who lived in the tree near her house. She climbed up to his branch and posed her question. Hootie blinked his big round eyes and hooted softly before sharing his guess. "I believe the moon changes shape because it is telling a story to the world," he said. Nia listened intently, imagining the moon weaving tales with its different shapes. She thanked Hootie for his answer and climbed back down to her garden, feeling excited to share all the guesses she had collected.

As Nia sat in her garden, she thought about all the different guesses she had gathered from her friends. She looked up at the moon, which was now a beautiful crescent shape in the night sky. Smiling to herself, she realized that maybe the moon changed shape simply because it wanted to. It was a magical mystery that would always be a part of the nighttime sky, twinkling down on her and everyone else in the world. With a contented sigh, Nia snuggled up in her blanket, feeling grateful for the wonders of the universe and the joy of curiosity. And as she drifted off to sleep, the moon continued to shine, watching over her like a silent guardian in the night.
