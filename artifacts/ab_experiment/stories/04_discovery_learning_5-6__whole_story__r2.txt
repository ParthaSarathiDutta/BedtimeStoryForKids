Once upon a time, in a cozy little house at the edge of a big, bustling city, there lived a sweet and curious little girl named Nia. Nia was five years old, with big, bright eyes that sparkled with wonder. Every night, just before bedtime, Nia would go out into her back garden with her mommy and daddy to look at the moon.

One night, as Nia gazed up at the sky, she asked her mommy, "Why does the moon change shape every night?"

Her mommy smiled and said, "Hmm, well, some people believe that the moon is playing hide-and-seek with the stars, so it changes shape to trick them."

Nia giggled at the thought of the moon playing games with the stars. The next night, she asked her daddy the same question. He thought for a moment and said, "I've heard that the moon is actually a giant cookie in the sky, and each night, a little piece gets nibbled away by a magical creature."

Nia's eyes widened in amazement at the idea of a cookie moon. She decided to ask her wise old neighbor, Mrs. Jenkins, for her opinion. Mrs. Jenkins chuckled and said, "Oh, my dear, the moon changes shape because it's dancing with the clouds, and each night, they twirl and spin in the sky, creating new shapes."

Nia was enchanted by Mrs. Jenkins' explanation. That night, as she drifted off to sleep, she whispered to the moon, "Thank you for dancing with the clouds and playing with the stars. I can't wait to see what shape you'll be tomorrow."

And as the moon shone down on Nia's garden, casting a soft, silver light over everything, she closed her eyes, feeling calm and content, knowing that the moon would always be there, changing shape night after night, just for her. Goodnight, sweet Nia. Sweet dreams.
