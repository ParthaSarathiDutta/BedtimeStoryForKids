Once upon a time, in a lush jungle filled with tall trees and colorful flowers, there lived a baby elephant named Tomo. Tomo was very shy, and he was especially scared of crossing the wobbly rope bridge that led to the other side of the river where his herd liked to play and eat juicy fruits.

One sunny day, as the rest of the elephant herd gathered by the river, ready to cross the bridge, Tomo hesitated. His heart raced with fear as he watched the bridge sway in the gentle breeze. He knew he couldn't stay behind alone, so he took a deep breath and slowly stepped onto the bridge, one shaky foot at a time.

As Tomo started to walk, the bridge wobbled beneath his weight, making him even more nervous. His trunk curled up in anxiety, but he kept going, looking straight ahead at the other side where his family and friends were waiting for him. The herd encouraged him with soft trumpets and gentle nudges, showing him that they believed in him.

Just when Tomo was starting to feel a little more confident, a mischievous monkey swung down from a tree and landed on the bridge, causing it to shake and sway wildly. Tomo froze in fear, feeling like he couldn't take another step. But then, he remembered the reassuring presence of his herd behind him and the thought of reaching the other side where he would be safe and happy.

With renewed determination, Tomo took a few more steps, his heart pounding in his chest. He focused on his breathing and the sound of the river below, trying to block out the monkey's antics. Slowly but surely, he made his way across the bridge, feeling the support of his family every step of the way.

Finally, with a sigh of relief, Tomo reached the other side of the river. The herd greeted him with joyful trumpets and loving nuzzles, proud of his bravery. Tomo felt a warm sense of accomplishment wash over him as he realized that he had found his courage, one small step at a time.

As the sun began to set and the jungle grew quiet, Tomo snuggled up with his family, feeling safe and loved. And as he drifted off to sleep, he knew that no matter what challenges lay ahead, he could always count on the love and support of his elephant herd. Goodnight, little one.
