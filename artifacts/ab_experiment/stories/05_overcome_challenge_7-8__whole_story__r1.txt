Once upon a time, in a lush jungle filled with towering trees and colorful flowers, there lived a shy young elephant named Tomo. Tomo was always hesitant and unsure of himself, especially when it came to doing things that scared him. But Tomo had a big heart and a strong desire to prove himself to his family and friends in the herd.

One sunny day, the elephant herd needed to cross a wide river to reach the other side of the jungle. The only way to cross was a wobbly rope bridge that swayed gently in the breeze. Tomo's heart raced with fear at the sight of the bridge, but he knew he had to find his courage and join his family on the other side.

With a deep breath, Tomo took his first step onto the bridge. The ropes creaked and groaned under his weight, causing him to freeze in fear. His family watched from the other side, urging him on with gentle trumpets and reassuring nods. Tomo closed his eyes and took another step, then another. Each step was a challenge, but he kept pushing himself forward, one small step at a time.

Just as Tomo was starting to feel a bit more confident, a sudden gust of wind shook the bridge violently. Tomo stumbled and nearly fell, his heart pounding in his chest. But instead of giving up, Tomo took a deep breath and focused on the sound of his family's encouraging voices. With renewed determination, he continued across the bridge, his steps becoming more steady and sure with each passing moment.

Finally, after what felt like an eternity, Tomo reached the other side of the river. His family greeted him with joyful trumpets and loving nuzzles, proud of his bravery and perseverance. Tomo's heart swelled with happiness and pride as he realized that he had found his courage in the face of fear.

As the sun began to set, the elephant herd continued on their journey through the jungle, with Tomo walking confidently by their side. The gentle rustle of the trees and the soothing sounds of the river filled the air, wrapping Tomo in a warm blanket of safety and love.

And as Tomo settled down for the night, surrounded by his family under the starry sky, he knew deep in his heart that he could overcome any challenge that came his way, one small step at a time. Goodnight, Tomo. Sleep well, brave little elephant.
