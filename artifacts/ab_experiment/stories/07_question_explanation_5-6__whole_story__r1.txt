Once upon a time, on a warm summer evening, Theo sat on his grandmother's porch, watching the sun slowly dip below the horizon. As the sky turned a beautiful shade of blue, Theo turned to his grandmother and asked, "Why is the sky blue?"

His grandmother smiled and took his hand. "Well, Theo, some say the sky is blue because of the ocean reflecting its color. Others say it's because of the way the light from the sun scatters in the atmosphere."

Theo's eyes widened with wonder. "But why do you think the sky is blue, Grandma?"

His grandmother chuckled softly. "I like to think that the sky is blue because it's the color of peace and calm. When we look up at the blue sky, it reminds us to be still and breathe in the beauty of the world around us."

Theo looked up at the sky, feeling a sense of peace wash over him. "I like that, Grandma. It makes me feel happy."

His grandmother hugged him tightly. "I'm glad, Theo. Just remember, no matter what the reason may be, the sky is always there to watch over us and remind us of the beauty in the world."

As the stars began to twinkle in the night sky, Theo snuggled close to his grandmother, feeling safe and loved. And as he drifted off to sleep, he knew that the sky would always be there, shining down on him with its calming, blue light.
