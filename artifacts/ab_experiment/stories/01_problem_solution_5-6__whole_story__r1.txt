Once upon a time in a sunny meadow, there lived a small hedgehog named Pip. Pip loved her cozy blanket, which was the softest and warmest blanket in the whole meadow. But one day, just before nap time, Pip couldn't find her favorite blanket anywhere.

Pip started to worry. She searched high and low, under bushes and behind trees, but her blanket was nowhere to be found. Tears welled up in Pip's eyes as she asked for help from her friends in the meadow.

The animals of the meadow gathered around Pip, determined to help her find her precious blanket. The birds flew up high to look from above, the rabbits hopped around searching the ground, and the squirrels scurried up and down the trees.

After a thorough search, the meadow animals finally found Pip's blanket caught on a thorny bush. They carefully untangled it and brought it back to Pip. She hugged her blanket tightly, feeling grateful for her friends' help.

With her blanket back in her arms, Pip snuggled down for her nap, surrounded by all her friends in the meadow. The sun shone down, warming her as she drifted off to sleep, feeling safe and loved.

And from that day on, Pip always made sure to keep her blanket close by, knowing that she was surrounded by friends who would always be there to help her. Goodnight, little one.
