Once upon a time, in an old stone castle, a baby dragon lived in the garden. The dragon was small, with shiny scales and bright, curious eyes. The castle was a peaceful place, with flowers blooming and birds singing, but one day, the baby dragon went missing.

Mira, a young knight-in-training, heard the news and knew she had to help. She dressed in her shining armor, grabbed her sword, and called for her loyal owl, Luna. Together, they set off to find the missing dragon before dark.

Through the castle gates and into the forest they went, following the dragon's footprints. Mira and Luna searched high and low, calling out for the baby dragon. They faced obstacles along the way - a wild river to cross, a dark cave to explore, but they never gave up.

As the sun began to set, Mira and Luna finally found the baby dragon. It was stuck in a thicket, trying to free itself. With gentle hands, Mira helped the dragon out, and it chirped happily, flapping its wings.

Together, they hurried back to the castle, the baby dragon flying happily above them. The castle was bathed in the warm glow of the setting sun as they returned, the dragon safe and sound. The king and queen were overjoyed and thanked Mira and Luna for their bravery.

That night, as Mira lay in her bed, Luna perched on the windowsill. The stars twinkled in the sky, and a gentle breeze rustled the curtains. Mira knew that she had done a good deed, and she drifted off to sleep with a smile on her face.

And as she slept, Luna hooted softly, keeping watch over the castle and its inhabitants. The night was calm and peaceful, filled with dreams of adventures yet to come. And in the morning, Mira and Luna would rise once again, ready for whatever challenges the day might bring. Goodnight, little one. Sweet dreams.
