Once upon a time, in an old stone castle, there lived a young knight-in-training named Mira. She had always dreamed of becoming a brave knight like the ones in the stories her grandmother told her each night. One day, as Mira was practicing her sword skills in the castle gardens, she noticed that the baby dragon was missing.

The baby dragon was a mischievous little creature who loved to explore the castle grounds. Mira knew she had to find the dragon before it got dark. She called out to her loyal owl, who perched on a nearby tree, and together they set off on their adventure.

As they searched the castle gardens, Mira and her owl encountered many obstacles. They had to climb over fallen branches, cross a rushing stream, and navigate through a dark and eerie forest. But Mira was determined to find the baby dragon and bring it safely back home.

Finally, as the sun began to set and darkness started to creep in, Mira and her owl spotted the baby dragon. It was perched on top of a tall tower, looking scared and lonely. Mira knew she had to be brave and climb up to rescue the dragon.

With the help of her owl, Mira climbed higher and higher until she reached the top of the tower. She gently picked up the baby dragon and cradled it in her arms. The dragon looked up at her with big, grateful eyes, and Mira knew that they had formed a special bond.

Together, Mira, her owl, and the baby dragon made their way back to the castle. The other knights and castle residents cheered as they saw Mira's bravery and kindness in rescuing the dragon. The baby dragon was returned safely to its mother, and Mira felt a warm sense of pride and accomplishment.

As night fell over the castle, Mira and her owl sat by the fire, sharing stories of their adventure. The castle was peaceful and quiet, and Mira knew that she had found her place as a true knight. With a contented smile on her face, she bid her owl goodnight and drifted off to sleep, knowing that she had done a good deed and made new friends in the process. And as the stars twinkled in the night sky, Mira knew that she was exactly where she belonged. Goodnight, sweet dreams, and remember, even in the dark, there is always light.
