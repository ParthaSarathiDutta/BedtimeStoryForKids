Once upon a time, in a castle surrounded by tall stone walls and a lush garden, there lived a young knight-in-training named Mira. Mira was brave and kind, with a heart as warm as the sun. She spent her days practicing sword fighting and learning about chivalry from the wise old knight who lived in the castle.

One sunny morning, as Mira was wandering through the castle garden, she heard a loud roar. Startled, she looked around and saw that the baby dragon who lived in the garden was missing. Without hesitation, Mira knew she had to find the dragon and bring it back home before dark.

Mira's loyal owl, who was always by her side, hooted softly and flew down to land on her shoulder. Together, Mira and her owl set off on their adventure to find the missing dragon. They searched high and low, through the forest and over the hills, calling out for the dragon with every step they took.

As they journeyed on, they faced many obstacles. They crossed a rushing river on a rickety old bridge, climbed a steep mountain, and even braved a dark cave filled with bats. But Mira and her owl never gave up hope. They knew they had to find the baby dragon and bring it back to safety.

Finally, as the sun began to set and the sky turned a deep shade of pink, Mira and her owl spotted the baby dragon. It was perched on a cliff overlooking the castle, its small wings fluttering in the breeze. With a brave heart, Mira approached the dragon slowly, speaking softly to calm its fears.

The baby dragon looked at Mira with big, innocent eyes and let out a happy purr. It nuzzled against her hand, grateful to be found. Mira smiled and gently picked up the dragon, cradling it in her arms.

Together, Mira, her owl, and the baby dragon made their way back to the castle, where they were greeted with cheers and applause. The wise old knight praised Mira for her bravery and kindness, and the baby dragon snuggled happily in its warm nest in the garden.

As night fell over the castle, Mira tucked herself into bed, her owl perched on the windowsill, and the baby dragon curled up at the foot of her bed. With a contented sigh, Mira closed her eyes, knowing that she had brought her friends safely home. And as she drifted off to sleep, she knew that no matter what adventures tomorrow might bring, she would always be there to protect and care for them. Goodnight, brave Mira. Goodnight, loyal owl. Goodnight, baby dragon. Sweet dreams.
