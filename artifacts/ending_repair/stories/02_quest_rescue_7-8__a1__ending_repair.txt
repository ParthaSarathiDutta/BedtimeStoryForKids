Once upon a time, in a grand old stone castle, there lived a young knight-in-training named Mira. Mira was brave and kind, always ready to help those in need. She spent her days practicing with her sword and shield, dreaming of the day she would become a true knight.

One sunny morning, as Mira was wandering through the castle garden, she noticed that the baby dragon who lived there was missing. The dragon was a mischievous little creature, always getting into trouble, but Mira loved her all the same. Without hesitation, she knew she had to find the dragon and bring her back before dark.

Mira's loyal owl companion, Luna, hooted softly and flew down to her side. Together, they set off on their journey to find the missing dragon. They searched high and low, through the forest and across the fields, asking every creature they met if they had seen the baby dragon.

As the sun began to sink lower in the sky, Mira and Luna came upon a deep, dark cave. They could hear the faint sound of whimpering coming from inside. With a deep breath, Mira bravely entered the cave, Luna by her side.

Inside, they found the baby dragon, trapped between some rocks. She had gotten herself stuck while trying to explore the cave. Mira gently freed the dragon, who nuzzled her gratefully. Together, they made their way back to the castle, just as the stars began to appear in the night sky.

The king and queen were overjoyed to see the baby dragon safe and sound. They thanked Mira for her bravery and kindness, and the dragon curled up contentedly in her arms. Luna hooted happily from her perch, proud of her friend's courage.

That night, as Mira lay down to sleep in her cozy room in the castle, Luna perched on the windowsill, watching over her. The baby dragon snuggled close, her warm scales glowing softly in the moonlight. Mira drifted off to sleep, knowing that she had done a good deed and that her friends were safe and sound by her side.

As the moon shone brightly through the window, casting a gentle glow over the room, Mira felt a sense of contentment wash over her. Luna's soft hoots and the baby dragon's gentle purring filled the air with a soothing lullaby. Snuggled up with her friends close by, Mira closed her eyes and drifted off into a peaceful sleep, knowing that she was safe and loved in the warmth of the castle. And so, in the stillness of the night, dreams of adventure and friendship danced through her mind, guiding her into a restful slumber until the new day dawned.
